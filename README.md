## 🔒 Privacy Policy
*Last updated: September 2026*

Your privacy is critically important to us. **Budle** is designed with a privacy-first approach:

* **Local Storage Only:** All data, information, or content you create and input into the app is stored strictly on your local device. 
* **No Internet Transmission:** The app does not transmit, upload, or share your data over the internet to any external servers, third parties, or databases.
* **No Tracking:** We do not collect, track, or monitor your personal information, usage analytics, or behavioral data.
